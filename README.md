## ⚡ What Does It Do?

Many older TVs and budget Smart TVs cannot play modern video files properly:
- ❌ Audio doesn't work (E-AC3, Dolby Digital not supported)
- ❌ Subtitles don't show (external .srt files ignored)
- ❌ 4K movies look stretched or cut off on 1366×768 screens
- ❌ 21:9 ultra-wide movies have no black bars

**AlphaTV Converter fixes all of this automatically in one click.**

---

## ✅ Features

- 🎬 **Burns subtitles** directly into the video (SRT, ASS, SSA supported)
- ⬛ **Adds letterbox black bars** — subtitles display in the black bar, not over the picture
- 🔊 **Fixes audio** — converts any audio format to stereo AAC for maximum TV compatibility
- 📺 **Custom TV resolution** — works for any TV (1366×768, 1920×1080, and more)
- ⚡ **GPU acceleration** — NVIDIA NVENC for ultra-fast conversion
- 🖥️ **CPU fallback** — works on any PC even without a dedicated GPU
- 📏 **Target file size** — set a max output size and the app calculates the bitrate automatically
- ⏸️ **Pause / Stop** — full control over the conversion process
- 🔔 **Notifications** — get a sound alert when your movie is ready
- 🔄 **Auto-update checker** — get notified when a new version is released

## 📥 Download & Install

👉 **[Download the latest installer from Releases](https://github.com/AlphaK911/AlphaTV-Converter/releases/latest)**

1. Download `Setup_AlphaTV_Converter.exe`

## 🙋 FAQ

**Q: My TV has no sound after converting. What do I do?**  
A: Make sure you used AlphaTV Converter — it automatically converts all audio to stereo AAC which is compatible with all TVs.

**Q: The conversion crashed immediately. What do I do?**  
A: Click the **⚠️ Driver / Setup Help** button in the app and switch from GPU to CPU mode. This fixes 99% of errors.

**Q: Will this work on my 1080p TV?**  
A: Yes! Change the resolution to `1920 x 1080` in the settings before converting.

**Q: How long does conversion take?**  
A: Standard 1080p movies convert in 15–20 minutes. 4K HDR movies take 35–45 minutes. Your GPU makes it significantly faster.
